Your technical assessment, start to finish

A real bug in a real codebase — not a quiz. You'll fix it however you normally work, then explain what you did. This page shows exactly what happens at each step, so nothing catches you out.

About 60 minutes · Chrome or Edge · mic + camera

Part 1 — Build

Fix the code, your way

Your editor, your pace. Nothing is timed and nothing is watched.

AI tools welcome

Part 2 — Explain

Talk us through it

Fullscreen, on the clock, and you can't return to your code.

No AI, no going back

That split is the whole point. We let you use AI to write the fix because that's how the job actually works — so what we measure is whether you understand what you shipped. If you do, Part 2 is easy.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Install the DevSimulate extension

    Open VS Code, search DevSimulate in Extensions, install it, then sign in with GitHub.

  2. 2

    Open your ticket

    Your assigned ticket appears in the DevSimulate sidebar with the problem description and the files worth investigating.

    Read it twice. The ticket describes a symptom. Your job is to find the cause behind it — that's the largest part of your score.
  3. 3

    Click "Fork & Clone"

    This copies the repository to your GitHub account and downloads it. No git commands needed — work only in that copy.

  4. 4

    Fix the issue

    Take the time you need. Use Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT — whatever you'd normally reach for. Nothing here is monitored.

    Make it a minimal fix. A small change you can explain beats a large one you can't.
  5. 5

    Click "Push & Create PR"

    Pushes your branch and opens the pull request for you.

  6. 6

    Click "Submit PR for Review"

    This is the doorway into Part 2. Your browser opens and the monitored portion begins.

    Pause here. Once you continue, you cannot go back to your code, your notes, or any AI tool. Don't click until you can explain your fix from memory.
  7. 7

    Write it up — four questions

    In your own words:

    1. Root cause — what was actually broken, and why?
    2. How you found it — what you read, ran or tested.
    3. Why this fix — why this approach, what trade-off you accepted.
    4. How you verified it — what proved it works.

  8. 8

    Two follow-up questions

    Generated from your actual change, so they're specific to what you did. Short answers are fine.

  9. 9

    Explain it out loud

    A short spoken explanation with your camera and mic on. There's a microphone check first — please run it.

    Speak naturally and take your time. We're listening for whether you understand your change. Accent and grammar are never marked — a right explanation in imperfect English beats a fluent wrong one.
  10. ✓

    Done

    You'll see a confirmation. The hiring team reviews everything and comes back to you by email either way.

    You won't see a score — nobody does. That's deliberate for hiring assessments, so please don't read anything into it.

What's monitored in Part 2 — and what it costs

Fullscreen stays on Switching tabs or apps, or leaving fullscreen, is recorded. 2 warnings → a 3rd ends the assessment
Pasting is disabled in the answer boxes You'll get a warning if you try. Repeated attempts get looked at by a person. never ends your assessment — Ctrl+V is muscle memory
Camera on during the spoken part For presence only. Nothing else is inspected. no effect on your score

If something goes wrong

I clicked submit and nothing seems to be happening

Wait — reviewing your code takes up to a minute. Don't click submit again. If the page does come back to you, it's safe to retry; we won't double-count it.

My microphone isn't working

Don't close the tab. You'll be offered a retry, or the option to type your explanation instead. A broken mic never counts against you.

My tab closed / my laptop died mid-assessment

Sign back in and open your dashboard. You'll see a card returning you to exactly the step you left off on. Your written work is saved.

I got a warning by accident

One or two warnings are nothing to panic about. They exist so nobody is disqualified without knowing. Return to fullscreen and carry on.

My link has expired

Reply to your invitation email and someone will sort it out.

Nothing here is designed to catch you out. The rules exist so that every candidate's explanation is genuinely their own — including yours. Good luck.
